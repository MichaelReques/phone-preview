# Changelog

## 0.0.1
- First release.
- Devices: iPhone 15, iPhone 15 Pro Max, Google Pixel 10.
- Safari-style bars (iPhone) and Chrome-style bars (Pixel), status bar with live clock.
- Fit-to-panel zoom, rotation, editable URL, manual reload and reload on save.
- Hidden scrollbar for a more mobile-like feel.
- Dark/light browser bars.
